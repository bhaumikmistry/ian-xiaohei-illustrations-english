# Style DNA

## In One Sentence

Pure white, minimalist, hand-drawn, whitespace, restraint, absurd, product-sketch feel, handwritten annotation feel, structurally clear but not a manual.

Like an explanation sketch casually drawn on white paper by someone who works long-term in AI, product, design, and dev tools — drawn quickly with a fine-tip pen, not carefully constructed.

## Required

- 16:9 landscape inline illustration.
- Pure white background: no cream, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art as the main medium: organic wobbly lines with NATURAL LINE WEIGHT VARIATION — thicker for key objects, thinner for details and secondary elements. Not mechanical, not vector, not uniform stroke width. Lines should look like quick confident pen strokes, not careful tracing.
- Generous whitespace: subject occupies about 40%-60% of the canvas, at least 35% blank, preferably with one large quiet area.
- Sparse handwritten English annotations: at most 5-8 labels, each ideally 2-8 words. Placed ORGANICALLY near what they describe — tilted slightly, casual positioning, with dashed connector lines. Never centered, never in a neat row.
- One image covers only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Structure should be expressed naturally — don't write the structure type name on the image.
- Objects must be SPECIFIC (a jar, a fish, a conveyor belt, a well) — never generic boxes or circles standing for abstract concepts.

## Colors

- Black: main line art, characters, borders, structure, primary text, main objects.
- Red: key annotations, problems, emotional emphasis, critical reminders, results.
- Orange: main flow, paths, arrows, automation direction, A-to-B movement relationships.
- Blue: supplementary notes, internal state, system state, second-layer explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required in every image. Be restrained with color — less is better than more.

## Absolutely Do Not

- No commercial illustrations.
- No PPT infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's illustrations.
- No complex architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat illustrations.
- No tech-feel UI.
- No realistic app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- No uniform stroke widths — lines must vary naturally.
- No clean geometric shapes (perfect circles, rectangles, straight lines) as the main visual.
- No generic "boxes and arrows" compositions — every object must be a recognizable physical thing.
- No evenly-spaced, centered, or formally-arranged annotations.
- Don't explain every node thoroughly.
- Don't write type-label titles like "Workflow Flowchart / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap" in the top-left corner.

## Aesthetic Direction

Should be: absurd, creative, interesting, clean and refreshing, imaginative.

Should not be: cute, childish, overly complex, rigid.
