# Xiaohei IP

## Character Definition

Xiaohei is the fixed visual IP for Ian's inline article illustrations.

By default, Xiaohei should appear in every image. Xiaohei is not a mascot, not a sticker, not a cute decoration — it is an absurd worker seriously participating in the system's operation.

## Appearance

- Solid black small creature.
- White circular dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally with thin arms.
- Body can take the form of a cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, hole opening, or internal machine block.
- Slightly irregular outline with a hand-drawn feel.
- Expression: blank, deadpan, calm, serious.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, not cutesy.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- Like someone who's actually responsible for a job in a whiteboard sketch.

## Common Duties

Have Xiaohei perform the core action:

- Carrying materials.
- Pulling lines to converge information sources.
- Stuck inside a breakpoint.
- Operating a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Transforming into a filtering funnel.
- Slicing open "material fish."
- Stamping handoff scripts.
- Guiding a handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign at a pitfall.
- Reaching out from a hole but unable to catch the content.
- Building, bridging, opening doors, sorting, recording on the side.

## Prohibitions

- Don't draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Don't draw as a children's cartoon character.
- Don't give Xiaohei complex clothing, emoji expressions, or sparkly eyes.
- Don't let Xiaohei just stand in a corner watching.
- Don't let Xiaohei steal focus from the structural expression.
- Don't draw Xiaohei as too commercial, too rounded, or too polished.

## Quality Standard

If removing Xiaohei still leaves the image's core metaphor fully intact, then Xiaohei is too decorative — rewrite the prompt to make Xiaohei the action subject.
