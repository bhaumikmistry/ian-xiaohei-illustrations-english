## Side-by-Side Comparison Test

### Both Characters in One Theme: "Trust Building"

**Xiaohei version:**
```text
Generate one standalone 16:9 horizontal illustration. Pure white background. Minimalist black hand-drawn line art. Slightly wobbly pen lines. Lots of empty white space.

Xiaohei, a small solid-black creature with white dot eyes and thin legs, is carefully placing small blocks (labeled "proof") one by one to build a bridge between two cliffs. Xiaohei is on hands and knees, seriously and carefully placing the next block. The bridge is half-finished. Red annotation: "not shouting". Blue annotation: "piece by piece". Orange arrow showing direction of progress. Absurd, deadpan, clean.
```

**Haku version:**
```text
Generate one standalone 16:9 horizontal illustration. Pure white background. Minimalist black hand-drawn line art. Slightly wobbly pen lines. Lots of empty white space.

Haku, a tall floating spirit with black fading robe, white oval mask (dark eyes, mouth mark, grey tear marks), dark hands extended — hovering in the gap between two cliffs. Haku's body IS the bridge. Small items (evidence, proof) flow through Haku from one cliff to the other. Haku is the conduit of trust, not building it manually but embodying the transfer. Orange flow arrows through Haku's body. Red annotation: "trust flows through". Clean, mysterious, quiet, absurd.
```
